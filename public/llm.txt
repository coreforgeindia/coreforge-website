## Company Summary
CoreForge is an execution-driven technology and practical engineering company based in Bengaluru, India. Founded in October 2024, CoreForge bridges the gap between academic engineering theory and real-world industrial execution across embedded hardware, PCB design, custom software platforms, technical workshops, and developer mentoring.

## Key Capabilities & Verticals

### 1. Hardware Design & Embedded Systems
- Electronics Product Design: Circuit design, simulation, component selection, BOM optimization, rapid prototyping, Design for Manufacturing (DFM).
- PCB Design Services: Schematic capture, 2-layer to 8+ layer multi-layer PCB layout, high-speed PCB design, RF routing, design rule checks, Gerber file generation.
- Embedded Systems & Firmware: Microcontroller programming on STM32, ESP32, Arduino, and ARM Cortex architectures. FreeRTOS task scheduling, device drivers, low-power modes, secure OTA firmware updates.
- End-to-End Development: From requirement analysis to fabrication support, assembly management, and functional validation.

### 2. Software & Tech Solutions
- Custom Software Development: Tailored ERP and CRM systems for manufacturing, retail, logistics, and education.
- Modern Web Development: Fast, responsive, SEO-optimized business websites, SaaS web applications, and customer portals using React, Next.js, and modern CSS.
- Mobile App Development: Native and cross-platform mobile apps for Android and iOS using React Native and Flutter.
- Business Intelligence: Interactive dashboards, telemetry data visualization, KPI analytics, and automated reporting tools.

### 3. Workshops, Training & DIY Kits
- Technical Bootcamps: Hands-on programs in Embedded Systems, Embedded C, STM32, ESP32, IoT, and PCB Design (KiCad / Altium).
- Corporate & Academic Programs: Faculty Development Programs (FDPs), college workshops, student hackathons, and structured internships.
- DIY Electronics Kits: Beginner kits, IoT sensor kits, robotics modules, and embedded learning packs with step-by-step documentation.
